Colours in Turkish

Learn colour words, adjective order and useful sentences for describing clothes, objects and landscapes.

Essential colours

kırmızı — red

mavi — blue

sarı — yellow

yeşil — green

beyaz — white

siyah — black

turuncu — orange

mor — purple

pembe — pink

kahverengi — brown

Build a sentence

The adjective comes before the noun and does not agree:

Pattern
kırmızı bir elma — a red apple
mavi gömlek — a blue shirt

Hangi rengi seviyorsun?
Which colour do you prefer?

Bu elbise çok güzel.
This dress is very beautiful.

Shades and harmony

açık means light and koyu means dark:

açık mavilight blue
koyu yeşildark green

Use -rengi for “coloured”: altın rengi = golden, gümüş rengi = silver.
